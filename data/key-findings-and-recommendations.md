# Key Findings and Recommendations

# Key Findings

## 1. Content Performance Was Highly Concentrated

Approximately 20% of uploads generated nearly 90% of total views.

### Recommendation
Focus on identifying and replicating high-performing content structures instead of increasing upload quantity alone.

---

## 2. Long-Form Content Dominated Viewership

Long-form content generated approximately 91.69% of total views.

### Recommendation
Continue prioritizing long-form storytelling and promotional formats to maximize audience reach.

---

## 3. Shorts Had Lower Overall Contribution

Shorts generated approximately 8.31% of total views.

### Recommendation
Use Shorts primarily for awareness, audience discovery, and promotional support rather than as the main engagement driver.

---

## 4. Mid-Length Videos Performed Best

Videos between 1–5 minutes achieved the strongest average performance.

### Recommendation
Optimize content around concise and high-retention formats to improve engagement efficiency.

---

## 5. Moderate Metadata Usage Performed Better

Moderate tag counts outperformed excessive tagging strategies.

### Recommendation
Maintain clean and relevant metadata instead of relying on heavy optimization techniques.

---

## 6. Longer Titles Showed Better Results

Longer and more descriptive titles generally achieved stronger engagement.

### Recommendation
Improve title clarity and descriptive storytelling to increase discoverability and click potential.

---

## 7. Upload Timing Influenced Results

Morning and afternoon uploads outperformed late-night publishing windows.

### Recommendation
Optimize publishing schedules around higher audience activity periods.

---

## 8. Upload Frequency Alone Was Not Enough

Higher upload volume did not automatically generate stronger performance.

### Recommendation
Prioritize scalable quality patterns and repeatable successful formats over quantity-focused publishing.

---

# Final Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following areas appear most important for scalable growth:

- Long-form content optimization
- Mid-length engagement-focused formats
- Better publishing schedules
- Cleaner metadata strategies
- Repeatable content structures
- Franchise/show-level performance analysis

The analysis highlights the importance of data-driven content strategy and performance-focused decision-making.
